// leif claesson audio dsp · small sharp tools

I write audio DSP. Everything on this page is a side effect.

Swedish, in Thailand since 2007. I have been writing broadcast audio processing since 1996, starting in fixed point on a 486SX with no FPU, which is still visible in how I write code.

It began as LC audio in Gothenburg: D2LIK, a 4-bit ADPCM stereo codec built to carry audio over IPX at demoscene LAN parties, then Ultimod, a DOS broadcast processor written in PowerBASIC with inline assembler. Since then: the Aeromax and Aero television processors at Linear Acoustic, Omnia.9 for Telos, and my own line, Breakaway.

None of that is on this page. What is here are the small Windows tools I built because I needed them and nobody else had, plus the occasional thing dug back out of a 30-year-old disk. All of it free.

Free to use. Closed source. Made because they needed to exist.

The tools are not products. They are the sharp little things that made a working day smoother, polished until they were worth handing out. So they are yours to use, at no cost, with no strings.

The source stays private, and that is deliberate: it is so rare that anyone actually helps a project, mostly they just fork it and make it their own. So use them freely. Just do not expect the guts.
